## STEP 1: Design **✨**

#### 1. Customize The Badge Design

* Here, you can select the ***fill type, background color, and shape*** to your preference.

#### 2. Select The Badge Position

First, choose display location:

* <mark style="color:blue;">**Inside product image**</mark>: The badge will ***overlay on*** the product image
* <mark style="color:blue;">**Outside product image**</mark>: The badge will ***appear outside*** the product image

Then, depending on your choice above, you'll see different positioning options:

<div><figure><img src="/files/9GtcvcaZU7xWu3OxHn8X" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="/files/FS4yU8U2Ls2hB1zGhQ9V" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 3. Edit The Badge Content

* In this step, you can type anything you want in the badge content, change the text font, or add translations for the content.
* Or if you want a dynamic content, select "***AutoText***" and select a suitable content.

<figure><img src="/files/Lro1JRj3rN2ZAUl4C3tp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Please take a look at important notes about "AutoText" [here](/deco-guidelines/our-special-features/add-dynamic-content.md)\

#### 4. Adjust The Badge Size

👉 Edit Shape Size:&#x20;

Choose 1 in 2 size options:

* <mark style="color:blue;">**Same size on all screens**</mark>: Width and height ***remain consistent*** across ***all devices and pages***

<figure><img src="/files/lDw24n0JnLwiY2Wv1r3j" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* <mark style="color:blue;">**Custom size per screen**</mark>: Set ***different dimensions*** for ***each device*** (Desktop/Mobile) and ***page type*** (Collection/Product page)

**Size Units:** Choose between Percentage (%) or Pixels (px)

<figure><img src="/files/SZoz9BGyJ7yvg8RR6dwV" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE**: Size settings for ***Collection Pages*** automatically apply to ***Home Page and Search Page.***
{% endhint %}

👉 Edit Text Font Size:

* <mark style="color:blue;">**Check for the box "Auto-adjust text size"**</mark>: Text size ***adjusts automatically*** in ***percentage (%)*** to fit within the shape
* <mark style="color:blue;">**Unchecked for the box "Auto-adjust text size"**</mark>: Set a ***fixed font size*** in ***pixels (px)*** for precise control.

## STEP 2: Products 🏷️

*(*&#x44;efine which products should display your badge&#x73;*)*

### Option I -  Specific products&#x20;

* **Manually select** individual products to showcase your badge.&#x20;

⇒ Choosing this option ensures the badge ***appears only on the selected items.***

{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE**: The app ***can't detect*** products ***in draft/archived status OR not published on the Sale channel***
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="/files/ZF4c9obWxEGPyf9nedHx" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="/files/FssfCdT1kKA2uELVWW5x" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Option II - All products&#x20;

* With 1-click, the badge will be applied to all active products.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/BDTorvVo7evsIlopMOO6" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Option III - Automated Conditions

<details>

<summary>Automatic conditions, which save much of your time</summary>

</details>

NOTE: Before selecting any conditions, please choose the matching condition first:&#x20;

* <mark style="color:blue;">**All conditions**</mark>: The badge will *<mark style="color:$info;">**ONLY APPEAR ON PRODUCTS**</mark>* that *<mark style="color:$info;">**MEET EVERY SINGLE CONDITION**</mark>* you selected in the app.
* <mark style="color:blue;">**Any conditions**</mark>: The badge *<mark style="color:$info;">**WILL APPEAR ON PRODUCTS**</mark>* that *<mark style="color:$info;">**MEET**</mark>**&#x20;AT LEAST 1 OF THE CONDITIONS*** you selected in the app.

<figure><img src="/files/q7cClIvvTi9QuEUx1GKv" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

👉 Example:&#x20;

Say you select these conditions:

* Product collection = "Electronics"
* In stock

**🛠️ With "All conditions":** A product must be in the Electronics collections **AND** be in stock to show the badge.

**🛠️ With "Any conditions":** A product **only needs to meet one requirement**—it could belong to the collection: Electronics **OR** be in stock to show the badge.

### ⚙️Basic Conditions

You can set up base on:

* The weight range,&#x20;
* The price range
* The product title&#x20;

&#x20;<mark style="color:blue;">⇒</mark> Y**our badge will automatically detect & show on products meeting the requirements.**

#### 11. Add a matching metafield

(*This will help connect your <mark style="color:blue;">**PRODUCT METAFIELD**</mark> with the <mark style="color:blue;">**DECO's BADGE**</mark> >> Then, <mark style="color:blue;">**show the badge**</mark> on <mark style="color:blue;">**products**</mark> with the <mark style="color:blue;">**matching value**</mark>*)

* First, click on the button "***+ Add metafield***" to start connecting

<figure><img src="/files/Ky0o9Pj2mrNvpi4vItnw" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Next, a list of all product metafields in your store will appear, ***check the corresponding box*** to select >> Then, ***click "Select"*** button

<figure><img src="/files/RJHpO4jTW8xMLBlCbsEm" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* After, **select either:**

<mark style="color:blue;">**Exists**</mark>: Show on **all products** that have the **metafield value filled in** (any value).

<mark style="color:blue;">**Equal to**</mark>: Show **only** on products with **the exact value entered**.

<figure><img src="/files/NMfyEY0ZtRTwihlvQnnP" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For example:&#x20;

<mark style="color:blue;">**↪ Selected option: Exists**</mark>

* *Metafield key*: `Plant type`
* *Option*: **Exists**
* *Result*: The badge will show on every product that has **any** `Plant type` value (e.g., "Indoor Only", "Outdoor", "Shade Loving", etc.).

<figure><img src="/files/k5PrtO5viaK9xq97pRDR" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<mark style="color:blue;">**↪  Selected option: Equal to**</mark>

* Metafield key: `Plant type`
* Option: **Equal to**
* Value entered: `Indoor Only`
* Result: The label will show **only** on products with `Plant type = Indoor Only`.

<figure><img src="/files/WtqBwAYReQlN7YuLFbOm" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
