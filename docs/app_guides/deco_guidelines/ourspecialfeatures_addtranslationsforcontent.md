# Add Translations For Content

## 🎐 Which Features Have This Translation Feature

* Badges
* Banners&#x20;
* Trust Badges
* Tooltips

## ✨ Step 1: Preparations - Set Up Languages in Shopify

*<mark style="color:$primary;">**(Skip this step if you've already configured multiple languages on your store.)**</mark>*

#### 1/ Access Language Settings

* Navigate to *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Settings**</mark>* in the bottom-left corner of your *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Shopify admin**</mark>*

<figure><img src="/files/fQmK1LHtFxzdpTWZMT3s" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 2/ Add Your Desired Languages

* Click on *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Languages**</mark>* >> *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Add a language**</mark>*
* *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Select the language(s)**</mark>* you want to display on your website
* Click *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Next**</mark>* to proceed

<div><figure><img src="/files/OadRT5wLufpvgfEQII6F" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="/files/4mbvGWciIgG2Q73mmCRD" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

<figure><img src="/files/0atlCUukt0dqwwoWncIE" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 3/ Enable and Publish

* Toggle the button to enable each language
* Click **Done**

<figure><img src="/files/rbg6AZkCahkPcn4VBlSR" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
**Important:** Your new languages will be in the status *<mark style="color:blue;">**"Not published"**</mark>* —don't forget to *<mark style="color:blue;">**publish them to make them live on your store**</mark>*
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="/files/DIgefG2Q4jQdeXjX03cd" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## ✨Step 2: Processing - On the DECO app

⇒ Now for the final step: Select your language(s) and add translations for your DECO badges, banners, and trust badges.

{% hint style="success" %}
Adding translations is <mark style="color:blue;">**available for all**</mark> DECO features, including: <mark style="color:blue;">**BADGE, BANNER, TRUST BADGE**</mark>
{% endhint %}

### 2. Add Translations For Content

*<mark style="color:blue;">**To provide a localized experience, follow the steps below for each feature type.**</mark>*

#### 🏅 For Badges:&#x20;

* Click the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**pencil icon**</mark>* to *<mark style="color:$primary;">**open**</mark>* your selected badge.

<figure><img src="/files/vgPWH2PwGid4FjWc7jPX" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Navigate to the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Design tab**</mark>* and find the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Content**</mark>* section.
* Click *<mark style="color:$primary;">**"Add translations"**</mark>* and *<mark style="color:$primary;">**select**</mark>* your *<mark style="color:$primary;">**target language.**</mark>*
* *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Enter**</mark>* *<mark style="color:$primary;">**your translated text**</mark>* and click "*<mark style="color:$primary;">**Add"**</mark>*.

<div><figure><img src="/files/0jmZnOPslhG7yF1DZvx6" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="/files/cZO0QdJYTZ0xdATygJVQ" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 🚩 For Banners:&#x20;

* Click the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**pencil icon**</mark>* to *<mark style="color:$primary;">**open**</mark>* your selected banner.

<figure><img src="/files/8CjPw2I4TEs9yIuiigYa" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* In the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Design tab**</mark>*, scroll to the bottom to find the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Translation**</mark>* section.
* Click *<mark style="color:$primary;">**"Add translations"**</mark>**,*** select a language, and enter your text.
* Click *<mark style="color:$primary;">**"Add"**</mark>* to save.

<div><figure><img src="/files/MnYMGtXVqaYWNF0dcqqj" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="/files/giwndQYyy72eapRBjLsg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 🛡️ For Trust Badges:

* Click the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**pencil icon**</mark>* to *<mark style="color:$primary;">**open**</mark>* your selected trust badge.

<figure><img src="/files/bRtQ5PEQLeWAKVdyFSO1" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* In the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Design tab**</mark>*, scroll to the bottom to find the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Translation**</mark>* section.
* Click *<mark style="color:$primary;">**"Add translations"**</mark>**,&#x20;**<mark style="color:$primary;">**select your language**</mark>*, and *<mark style="color:$primary;">**type the translation**</mark>*.
* Click *<mark style="color:$primary;">**"Add"**</mark>* to finish.

<div><figure><img src="/files/CrAoQ43lzLKmeXvyiHJn" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="/files/XLtsLB9ozWrxB9LFlrCP" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 🛡️ For Tooltips:&#x20;

Click the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**pencil icon**</mark>* to *<mark style="color:$primary;">**open**</mark>* your selected badge.<br>

<figure><img src="/files/ajhNskRsYV25dbOOKhRn" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Design tab**</mark>*, scroll to the bottom to find the *<mark style="color:$primary;">**Other Settings**</mark>* section.

* Here choose *<mark style="color:purple;">**Show tooltip for badge**</mark>*
* Click on *<mark style="color:purple;">**Add translations**</mark>*

<figure><img src="/files/J0of1jqpuH8YnWCaYbYL" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Here you can ***select your language*** and type the ***tooltip*** that you want.

<figure><img src="/files/zr6xN0PWSKR0PwAZHpwf" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can ***add as many translations*** as you want and also ***edit*** them here:&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/SSCqaTvxId9WQCsf9Gi4" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
