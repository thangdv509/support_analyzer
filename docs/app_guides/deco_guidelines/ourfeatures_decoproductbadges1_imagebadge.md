### III - Conditions

<details>

<summary>Automatic conditions, which save much of your time</summary>

</details>

NOTE: Before selecting any conditions, please choose the matching setting first:

* **All conditions**: The badge will <mark style="color:blue;">**ONLY APPEAR ON PRODUCTS**</mark> that <mark style="color:blue;">**SATISFY EVERY SINGLE CONDITION**</mark> you selected in the app.
* **Any conditions**: The badge <mark style="color:blue;">**WILL APPEAR ON PRODUCTS**</mark> that <mark style="color:blue;">**SATISFY AT LEAST 1 OF THE CONDITIONS**</mark> you selected in the app.

<figure><img src="/files/ZdX9HMHgz4RXF09SBDlW" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

👉 Example:

Say you select these conditions:

* Product collection = "Electronics"
* In stock

**🛠️ With "All conditions":** A product must be in the Electronics collections **AND** be in stock to show the badge.

**🛠️ With "Any conditions":** A product **only needs to meet one requirement**—it could belong to the collection: Electronics **OR** be in stock to show the badge.

### Basic Conditions

You can set up the weight range, price range & the product title in DECO, and the app **will automatically detect & show the badge on products meeting the requirements.**

#### 11. Add a matching metafield

(*This will help connect your <mark style="color:blue;">**PRODUCT METAFIELD**</mark> with the <mark style="color:blue;">**DECO's BADGE**</mark> >> Then, <mark style="color:blue;">**show the badge**</mark> on <mark style="color:blue;">**products**</mark> with the <mark style="color:blue;">**matching value**</mark>*)

* First, click on the button "***+ Add metafield***" to start connecting

<figure><img src="/files/L8sfFxKrbwfIdnstiLe7" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Next, a list of all product metafields in your store will appear, tick to select >> click **"Select"** button

<figure><img src="/files/eJqtXAvCkfpChk71as5F" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Then, **choose** between these 2 options:&#x20;

<mark style="color:blue;">**Exists**</mark>: Show on **all products** that have the **metafield value filled in** (any value).

<mark style="color:blue;">**Equal to**</mark>: Show **only** on products with **the exact value entered**.

<figure><img src="/files/OAIZs9cP2Fv2TIenUU7c" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For example:&#x20;

<mark style="color:blue;">**↪ Exists**</mark>

* *Metafield key*: `Plant type`
* *Option*: **Exists**
* *Result*: The label will show on every product that has **any** `Plant type` value (e.g., "Indoor Only", "Outdoor", "Shade Loving", etc.).

<figure><img src="/files/k5PrtO5viaK9xq97pRDR" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<mark style="color:blue;">**↪ Equal to**</mark>

* Metafield key: `Plant type`
* Option: **Equal to**
* Value entered: `Indoor Only`
* Result: The label will show **only** on products with `Plant type = Indoor Only`.

<figure><img src="/files/WtqBwAYReQlN7YuLFbOm" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<mark style="color:blue;">**→**</mark> Besides, if you want to <mark style="color:blue;">**show the value of the metafield on the label**</mark>, please visit [this metafield's common use cases](/deco-guidelines/our-special-features/product-metafields.md) 🥰
