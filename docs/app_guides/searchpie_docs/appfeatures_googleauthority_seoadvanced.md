# Google Search Console

This tab allows you to connect your **Google Search Console** account and view all detected issues in one place.\
Our app helps you detect and fix SEO issues directly from Google Search Console, so you can improve your site’s visibility, rankings, and overall search performance.

#### Step 1: Connect Google Search Console

Click the **CONNECT** button to link your Google Search Console account.

<figure><img src="/files/05W1EIa0lUNBLlPdrkNG" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
**Important notes:**

* Make sure you select the **same Google email** that was used to create or manage your Google Search Console property.
* You must **grant all requested permissions** during the authorization process.
  {% endhint %}

#### Step 2: Check Google Search Console issues

Once the connection is successful, the app will automatically fetch and display all Google Search Console issues for your store. In particular, the app focuses on issues from the following sections:

* **Indexing** – pages that are not indexed or have indexing problems
* **Shopping** – product and merchant-related issues affecting Google Shopping visibility
* **Enhancements** – structured data and rich result issues that impact search appearance

These insights help you quickly identify critical SEO problems and take action to improve your store’s performance in Google Search results.

<figure><img src="/files/c8eOuV0vSZbz2tIqybke" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 3: How can I help these Google Search Console issues?&#x20;

Google Search Console issues can be complex, especially those related to **indexing** and **structured data** requirements from Google.

Because of this complexity, resolving these issues often requires the involvement of a **technical SEO expert or developer** to properly analyze and fix them. Incorrect fixes may lead to further indexing or visibility problems.

Therefore, we highly recommend you signing up our **Google Search Console service.**&#x20;

Google Search Console service is a **one-time payment service, with 6-month warranty**. Our technical team can help you fix all the issues in Indexing, Shopping & Enhancement sections. Besides, during 6-month warranty, whenever you have new Google Search Console issues, our team can run the auto-tool to help you detect and fix the issue as soon as possible.&#x20;
