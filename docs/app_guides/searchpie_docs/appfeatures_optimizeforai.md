# Optimize for AI

In the era where AI is widely used across every aspect, applying AI to optimize SEO has become indispensable. Therefore, **Search Pie** provides a feature that allows you to improve your SEO performance with AI-powered tools.&#x20;

You can find this feature via the Optimize for AI tab:

<figure><img src="/files/2pbo4tMI40DMkO8oHy8p" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## AI Traffic

This feature allows you to track how much traffic your store receives from AI-powered platforms, including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity AI, and Microsoft Copilot.

To view this data, simply click the **Connect analytics** button and link your Google Analytics 4 account.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/faZl0WeSQjwhQTeFwCpr" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once connected, the dashboard will automatically display insights about the traffic coming to your store from these AI tools, including **total sessions,** **active users**, and **add to cart** figures, helping you better understand and optimize your store’s visibility in AI-driven search and recommendation systems.

<figure><img src="/files/9rO0lZJIGl9WJG3uM81u" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

If you would like to disconnect GA4, please click on the **Refresh** button, choose **Manage connection**

<figure><img src="/files/BWxB1LEMFBKKKZ2B6wsZ" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Then, the Connecting Google Analytics tab appears. Please click on the **Disconnect** button, and the GA4 will be disconnected successfully.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/ZfSHFM04ihX8RNhycUOc" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## LLM.txt file

LLM.txt is a text file placed in the root directory of a website (for example: `https://example.com/llm.txt`) that provides structured information about the website for Large Language Models (LLMs).

It helps AI systems understand the content, purpose, and important pages of a website when they access or analyze it.

Adding the LLM.text file will guide AI models in understanding and using the content of a website correctly.   &#x20;

In our app, we can guide you on creating a file or help you create one (for the Enterprise plan).

<figure><img src="/files/MTvYuO22XYHYJxAOPPLs" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

If you would like us to do so, simply click on the **Request adding file** button, and our Support team will give you further assistance regarding this.&#x20;
