# Keyword Audit

To further check the keyword presence on the important tags of your pages, such as Meta Title, Meta Description, etc, you can try the Keyword audit function. \
\
To use the **Keyword Audit** feature , you need to paste the **URL of your page** and **enter the keyword** that needs checking. \
\
In case if you don't know which keyword to enter, you can always click on the **Explore keyword** button that will take you to the Keyword Explore feature. There you can choose keyword base on Search Volume, Trend and Competition

<figure><img src="/files/NYSo9x9YC9EWnNlnYQWW" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After entering the URL and keyword, click **Audit:**

<figure><img src="/files/piXmDvROzdZvbTMvpcIr" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The result will be shown as follow:

<figure><img src="/files/6FrebiY2NrPCSGpFp3fS" alt=""><figcaption><p>Keyword Audit result.</p></figcaption></figure>

To have more information on the scan results, you can always click on the **dropdown arrow**. If you need help resolving or have any questions regarding the result, you can always **contact us for support.**<br>

After fixing the issues/notices, you can click **Rescan** to see if they are resolved.

<figure><img src="/files/l7oj81D6ILOPVFIYuZ8F" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To create a new keyword campaign, click on **Audit new keyword** and repeat the steps again to check:

<figure><img src="/files/2RxwwlA4g9kRi4DQ0Nqi" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The History section will contain your past keyword campaigns. Whenever you want to come back to working on a campaign, click **Optimize:**

<figure><img src="/files/5UEIdOkXd1Pd8bRSAxJY" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

If you want to remove the keyword campaign, you can click on the "trash" icon.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/6QJchNs4hOQmgoAj2NqL" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
