# Increase your product visibility on Google with SearchPie x BON Loyalty integration

Adding Loyalty schema helps search engines better understand your loyalty program (tiers, benefits, rewards). When your loyalty information is structured and consistent, it can support stronger visibility and eligibility for relevant search experiences over time.<br>

With the SearchPie x BON Loyalty integration, you can sync your loyalty program data from BON Loyalty into SearchPie and publish it as Loyalty schema, no manual copy-paste needed.

### Requirements to use SearchPie × BON Loyalty integration

To enable this integration, you must:&#x20;

1. On SearchPie’s Enterprise plan
2. On BON Loyalty’s Growth plan
3. You should have set up points and rewards programs for tiers on BON Loyalty.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** *This integration pulls loyalty program data from BON Loyalty (such as tiers and rewards) and uses it to generate Loyalty schema in SearchPie*
{% endhint %}

### How to set up SearchPie x BON Loyalty Integration.&#x20;

**Step 1:** On the Set up page for Loyalty schema, click Connect now. Or if you want hands-on assistance, choose Contact us to get direct guidance from our support team.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/e57tFB8rs00q3YyQ1trO" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2:** A confirmation modal will appear to ensure you approve syncing data between BON Loyalty and SearchPie. Choose the option that matches your situation:

**If BON Loyalty is already installed**

1. Click **Connect.**
2. SearchPie will begin syncing your BON Loyalty program data automatically.

<figure><img src="/files/GWnM9ZmIoXyQbMgZ2yFN" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**If BON Loyalty is NOT installed yet**

1. Click **Go to BON Loyalty**. You’ll be redirected to the BON Loyalty app listing.&#x20;
2. Install BON Loyalty first.&#x20;
3. Return to SearchPie and repeat Step 1 → Step 2 to complete the connection.

<figure><img src="/files/mDYHju82Tl7ucIumg6ds" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3:** After a successful connection, the integration status will show **Connected**. Your **tiers and rewards** will be automatically filled in the Loyalty schema setup. Review the information for accuracy. Click **Save** to activate the Loyalty schema.

<figure><img src="/files/6IYfFMxJBR3cDA7Z6M9h" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
