# AI Generation

AI Generation is another tool of SearchPie help you generate content of your page, including page title, page description, meta title, meta description, ALT tags and URL handle. The optimal point of this feature is that you have the help of AI tool, which helps you save lots of time, effort & resources on SEO optimization. This powerful feature is available for Premium and Enterprise plan.

The tool can help you generate content one by one or even in bulk. Let's go!&#x20;

Just click on the **AI Generate** tab in the SEO tags & Content feature.

<figure><img src="/files/HYVD9Er3sWpB6dNgXT2W" alt=""><figcaption><p>AI Generate.</p></figcaption></figure>

On the Product page, you can easily find your Product through our filters. Just simply click **Search & Filter**, then enter the product name you need, or add filter by status to find a product: Active or  Achieved.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/atc56duodKY4hYJY3fZ4" alt=""><figcaption><p>Search and filter.</p></figcaption></figure>

Also, if your **SEO element** of Product pages is **Long/short**, it means your content is longer/shorter than the standard. Don't hesitate, let's optimize right away!&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/cXQ0CR5VKXzzFFGaOypi" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**1. One-by-one AI generation:**&#x20;

To start optimizing the content of your page, let's click **Edit**

Here, we have the tool to check if your keyword is included in your important content. Just **Enter target keyword,** and click **Check** then you can see the result below:&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/IWgbMjzwfBbR9Yt27MEt" alt=""><figcaption><p>Enter target keyword.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="/files/Xze66K1HyqbwReyoyaCV" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

If you want to research which keyword is suitable with your business, please click **Find keyword,** it leads you to the [**Keyword Explore**](https://docs.searchpie.io/app-features/seo-tags-and-content/keyword-research) on our app to start researching and finding the best keyword for your store.

<figure><img src="/files/VJ8DZQRs4b1AJHwYZSpx" alt=""><figcaption><p>Find keyword.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="/files/PY9F3aThWd7kaOaHnluP" alt=""><figcaption><p>Keyword Explore</p></figcaption></figure>

And next, you can manually edit the content of your page, including page title, page description, meta title, meta description, Alt tags and URL handle.&#x20;

When editing, please pay attention to the Sample Preview on Google and the length standard. According to [Google's updates](https://mrs.digital/tools/meta-length-checker/#:~:text=Google%20changes%20the%20pixel%20length,to%20158%20characters%20on%20average), Google changes the pixel length of meta tags all the time. In 2024, for the best results, aim for between 10-60 characters for meta title and 50-170 characters for meta description.&#x20;

When it's done, please click **Save**.

<figure><img src="/files/mf9LMnzLNxObqDLOsMIr" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click Save when it's done.</p></figcaption></figure>

Especially, if you prefer using AI to save your time & effort while optimizing content, please click **Generate with AI** below the content you want to edit, then you have 2 options: **Generate with keywords**  and **Generate by rewriting the original meta description.**

<figure><img src="/files/IwkA0jO6nAuFGbTQ4tUH" alt=""><figcaption><p>AI Generate.</p></figcaption></figure>

If you click "**Generate by keywords**", the app will create your meta description based on the keywords on your meta title/description. What you have to do is enter your keywords and press **Enter**.&#x20;

Don't forget to choose the language and the writing tone you want AI to write, and finally click **Generate** button to see the results.

<figure><img src="/files/uH4NEQQugmu3p89Xvl4G" alt=""><figcaption><p>AI Generate - By keywords.</p></figcaption></figure>

In case you already have content and now want to generate the new one by AI, simply click **"Generate by rewriting the current"**. Next step, please choose your language and writing tones before generating.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/6uhlfzZaa8kBtQduMyKl" alt=""><figcaption><p>AI Generate - By rewriting the current.</p></figcaption></figure>

The app will scan for some seconds and generate the new content for you. If you're satisfied with that one, please **Apply** and then it will be applied as new content after you click **SAVE** button.

<figure><img src="/files/JMqYQN2di0Kt9xNwlYHS" alt=""><figcaption><p>Apply or Generate again.</p></figcaption></figure>

If you don't like the result, you can click **Generate again.** You have a maximum of **500 total request for Premium plan** and **5000 total requests for Enterprise plan** to generate AI. Each time you generate again, your total request will be deducted. If you want more AI request, please consider our [**AI VIP Generator**](https://docs.searchpie.io/pricing-plan/pricing-plans)

### 2. **Optimize SEO for Collection**

Optimize on-page SEO for the collection page by following the guide in Part 1. A key differentiator for the collection page is the "Text below item list".

The "Text below item list" on a collection page refers to keyword-rich content displayed below the list of items. This content enhances SEO rankings and optimizes the page's data structure. Find out more details [here](https://searchpie.io/why-you-should-include-text-below-your-collection-page/).

To display the text below the item list on your collection pages, you need to enable "SearchPie text below list" in the "App" section of your theme customization page.

**Step 1: Click "add a block to your theme" in SearchPie app to go to Shopify theme customization**.

<figure><img src="/files/o5PugpUJF3vUqM4iXjWL" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2: "SearchPie Text Below List" in the left sidebar.**

If you want to change its position on your collection page, simply drag the "App" section up or down to your desired location. Once you've positioned it, click the "Save" button at the top right to apply your changes.

<figure><img src="/files/C3yY7UMTur7SBQQY6iuQ" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
