# Long URLs

"How to create SEO-friendly URLs?" is a common question that many website owners face up to. There are different factors of link evaluation that search engines use, including the length of a link. A high-quality URL should NOT exceed the limit of 128 characters. Hence, the Long URLs feature was born to assist you in detecting too-long links and fixing them quickly.

<figure><img src="/files/3FOWa7C5mPCTmD9IQUWo" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You just need to click **Scan button,** and then the app will show you the result below:&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/sGKxaUM2gbHRYXWUGdmm" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

And if you want to edit those long URLs into shorter ones, let’s [**upgrade to the Enterprise plan**](https://apps.shopify.com/seo-booster) which allows you to break way more obstacles in your SEO journey! you can choose as many long URLs as you want then click on the “**Bulk Edit**” button and our app will help you shorten long URLs automatically. Or else, you can even click Edit to manually shorten the long URLs one by one.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/KoHP0L9MaHL10nd7M9ov" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can also edit the long URLs on your own by clicking the fix button next to the long URLs you want to shorten, then click Save.&#x20;

At the bottom, you can see the History of URLs shortened. If you don’t want to take them back, all you can do is click the revert button.
