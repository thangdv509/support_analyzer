Before we dive in, let's get to know [EComposer](https://shopify.pxf.io/e1NBZO) a bit. EComposer is a versatile, easy-to-use page builder designed for Shopify merchants. It enables you to create various page types or sections with a live drag-and-drop editor.&#x20;

Whether you need a landing page, homepage, collection page, product page, blog page, cart page, footer, coming soon page, 404 page, or any other theme section, EComposer has got you covered. It saves you time and money by offering built-in extensions to boost your sales.

Now that you're acquainted with EComposer, let's get started on building your SEO-friendly pages. Here are the steps to follow:

Step 1: Adding EComposer to Your Shopify Store

Install Ecomposer Landing Page Builder directly [here](https://shopify.pxf.io/e1NBZO). Once you've finished setup, go ahead and open up the EComposer dashboard.

Step 2: Building Your Pages

You'll discover two options to choose from that best suit your products and business. EComposer simplifies the process by offering flexibility in how you design your page.&#x20;

You can either build a unique page from scratch or make use of pre-made landing page templates available in the library. These templates can be customized by editing text and images to align with your specific product.&#x20;

EComposer provides a range of more than 20 pre-designed pages and numerous elements suitable for various fields. Feel free to select and drag-and-drop the most fitting template for your needs.

Step 3: Save and Publish Your Page

After finishing the text and images, click the 'Save and Publish' button to let your page go live.
