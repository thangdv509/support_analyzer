# Setting

1. **App Setups**

Don't forget to connect Google Search Console to SearchPie app to have full access to our helpful SEO features including Google Index and Position Tracking, that will improve the indexing rate of your webpage.

In case you have more than one Shopify store, you can connect all your stores here in SearchPie app to switch them easily.

Also, it is possible to sync all your app setups across connected stores by choosing which feature you want to set up.

Under the Connect multiple stores section is the place where you can change the language in apps. Currently, we provide many languages for our customers. We also have a plan to add more languages in the future.

### 2. Notifications

SearchPie provides you with a comprehensive **monthly report.** Data, which is collected based on your activities with our SEO app in 30 days, will be sent to your email on the 4th day of every month. The report includes updated information about SEO Score and solved SEO issues within the period.&#x20;

Highlighted news will be provided as well, focusing on SEO tips, new partners, and hot features on the app.

Just enter the email address you want to receive the monthly report in the box, or leave it blank if you want to receive it via your registered email.

## :question: FAQ About Email Report

{% hint style="info" %}

#### I upgraded to Enterprise Plan, why don’t I receive an email report from Search Pie on the 4th day of this month?

{% endhint %}

Please bear in mind that after you install our app, our email report doesn't send to users immediately. It aggregates your activities during your last 30 days. Due to that reason, it can only be sent on the 4th month if you have the app added for at least 30 days.
