# Speed Optimization

#### Why is speed important to your website?&#x20;

Website speed is essential for user experience. A fast-loading website helps visitors access content instantly, reduces bounce rates, and keeps users engaged longer. When pages load slowly, users are more likely to leave before taking any action, which can directly impact conversions and overall site performance.

**Speed also plays an important role in how Google evaluates your website.** Google considers page speed as a ranking factor and tends to crawl fast websites more efficiently. Faster pages are easier for Google to access, crawl, and index, which improves their chances of becoming visible in Google Search results and supporting long-term organic growth.

#### What is considered good website speed?&#x20;

A “good” website speed means your pages load quickly and smoothly for users, without delays or layout issues. In general, Google recommends that a website loads within **2–3 seconds** to provide a good user experience.

To evaluate your website speed, you can use [**Google PageSpeed Insights**](https://pagespeed.web.dev/). If your score is **50+ on mobile** and **70+ on desktop**, your website speed is considered good.

If your score is lower than these benchmarks, it indicates that your website may need further optimization to improve performance and user experience.
