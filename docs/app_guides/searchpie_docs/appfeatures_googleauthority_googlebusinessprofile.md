# Google Business Profile

In **Your Report**, our app will show you on the performance of your Google Business Profile in different aspects. You need to log in the email connecting to your Google Business Profile first, then your report will be shown.

<figure><img src="/files/GjurZINjqSfaIwsmEwcA" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Firstly, the app will show whether your Google Business Profile has enough information and how good it looks on website results & Google results.

<figure><img src="https://docs.searchpie.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F848413574-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252F-Mb9oMrqO590_abBSvwF%252Fuploads%252FVdJRhqaal2PbsFwX8OxX%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D5533bc3b-3a6f-40eb-8223-811a1c159777&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=c2226400&#x26;sv=1" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Next, **GBP Performance** shows you your current Google Business Profile's insight on a period, which can be based on performance & views. You can even change it to a specific period.

<figure><img src="/files/15fxbUdZDHusb0H8dGAj" alt=""><figcaption><p>GBP Performance</p></figcaption></figure>

To check your Google Business Profile's performance, we will evaluate the Overview, or detailed amount of Calls, Messages, Bookings, Directions, and Website clicks.

<figure><img src="/files/BLCIoeyKINsWrxbGkKH8" alt=""><figcaption><p>GBP Performance - Overview</p></figcaption></figure>

To check your Google Business Profile views, we will show you the number of people who viewed your Business Profile. The diagram below can give you detailed data from which keywords or devices visitors use to check your Google business profile.
