# App Dashboard

## SEO score

In our app dashboard, SearchPie will scan your whole site and show you the performance of your SEO score and the improvement of your store’s SEO over the past 7 days. The SEO score will include total issues, notices & good factors.

To start optimizing SEO, please click the **Optimize** button, then you'll go to SEO report to see the SEO performance of your Homepage.

## Google report overview

In the Google Report section, you will find key metrics that reflect your store’s SEO performance and product visibility on Google:

1. **Google PageSpeed**\
   This shows the loading speed of your homepage. A low score means your store needs optimization to improve user experience and SEO.
2. **Google Merchant Center**\
   This indicates the number of products that cannot be displayed in the Google Shopping Feed. Such issues directly affect your product exposure.
3. **Google Search Console**\
   This reports the number of products missing from Google Shopping ads. Fixing these issues will help ensure your products appear properly in ads.

With this report, you can quickly identify and resolve problems that impact your store’s SEO and Google performance.
